[h1]Round Report — Multiplayer Expedition Debrief[/h1]

[i]Who kept the sub running — and who nearly sank it?[/i]

Every round ends the same way for your crew now: a proper debrief. Round Report tallies who repaired what, who healed, who fought, who died, and crowns a round MVP with transparent, server-tuned scoring. No more arguing in chat about who carried.

[h2]Tracks everything that matters[/h2]
[list]
[*]Electrical and mechanical repairs
[*]Welding and hull maintenance
[*]Healing and CPR
[*]Firefighting
[*]Damage dealt and enemies killed
[*]Deaths and friendly fire
[*]Hull damage and reactor incidents
[/list]

[h2]The debrief[/h2]
At round end, the crew gets a native-style report window with:
[list]
[*]The MVP and their final score
[*]Contribution bars and job icons per player
[*]Team totals for every category
[*]Commendations for category leaders
[*]Incident call-outs for deaths, friendly fire and reactor failures
[*]Tabs for this round vs. campaign totals
[*]Score tooltips that show exactly how the MVP was calculated
[/list]

[h2]Stats that stick around[/h2]
Campaign totals persist across server restarts — stored per save, with an automatic backup. Switch campaigns and the stats switch with you. Round stats always start clean after a restart.

[h2]Tune the scoring to your crew[/h2]
Server owners decide what counts: weight repairs, welding, healing, firefighting, damage and kills however you like. Dial in death and friendly-fire penalties, and optionally disqualify reactor meltdowns from MVP entirely. Whatever the host sets is what every player's report window shows.

[h2]Commands[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]!stats[/b] — this round's crew report
[*][b]!mystats[/b] — your own stats for this round
[*][b]!total[/b] — saved campaign totals
[*][b]!rrversion[/b] — mod version and persistence diagnostics
[/list]

[h2]Languages[/h2]
English, Ukrainian, German, French, Polish, Russian, Brazilian Portuguese, Castilian Spanish, Latin American Spanish, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese.

The report window follows each player's own game language; server chat follows the server's. Unsupported languages fall back to English automatically.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*]Multiplayer only
[*]Barotrauma 1.13.4.0
[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2559634234]Lua For Barotrauma[/url] enabled on the host
[/list]

The graphical report window needs the client-side LuaCs install from the Lua For Barotrauma page — subscribing to Round Report alone doesn't add it. No LuaCs? No problem: you still get the full report in chat.
